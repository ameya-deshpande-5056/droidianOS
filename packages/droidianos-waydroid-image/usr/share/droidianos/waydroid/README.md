# Waydroid Defaults

The installer initializes embedded GAPPS system and MAINLINE vendor images. GAPPS includes Google Play services and the Google Play Store. Google may require device registration before Play Protect certification succeeds.

At first login, `droidianos-arm-setup` offers an explicit opt-in download for proprietary community ARM translation. Intel systems use Houdini and other x86-64 systems use libndk_translation. The pinned Android 13 payloads enable and verify ARM32 and ARM64 ABIs. They are downloaded to the installed machine and are not distributed in the ISO.
